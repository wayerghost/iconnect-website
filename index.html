About Us

About Us

i-Connect Student Society is a tech organization from University of Rizal System - Binangonan Campus. They focus on providing students essential materials such as seminars and other tech related events for the students.

Vision

i-Connect Student Society, as the sole computer-
based organization in the University of Rizal
System - Binangonan shall promote digital
literacy, technical competency and technological
capability among students that shall outgrow by
the year 2016.

Mission

i-Connect Student Society exists to provide supplementary knowledge in line with Information and Communication Technology, always promote computer courses that will unite the youth under the banner of leadership, technical excellence, and ethical conduct among themselves, while fostering community partnerships that enhance shared learning and social responsibility.

Everything you need to know about us.

iCSS is dedicated to bridging the gap between classroom learning and industry demands by hosting informative seminars, technical workshops, and skill-driven events that enhance digital literacy, technical excellence, and ethical leadership among CCS students. Through these initiatives, the society fosters a collaborative learning environment and community partnerships that empower future information technology professionals to excel in their careers.
Membership in the organization is ipso-facto, meaning that all students currently enrolled in the College of Computer Studies are automatically considered members of the i-Connect Student Society.
Every student member is required to contribute an organizational fee of Php 35 per semester. These collected collections go directly into the general fund of the i-Connect Student Society and are strictly used to finance all student-centered operations, including inviting expert speakers for technical seminars, purchasing materials for workshops, and organizing competitive, skill-driven events for the CCS community.
As an official member, you hold the right to be fully informed about all upcoming student projects, participate in any skill-driven events, receive organizational benefits, and inspect financial statements or records at any reasonable time. In exchange for these privileges, members share the responsibility to uphold the principles of the constitution, pay the necessary semestral dues on time, and actively cooperate and participate in all official meetings, consultations, and general assemblies called by the executive board.
When you join i-Connect Student Society activities, you can expect hands-on experiences that bring your classroom lessons to life and prepare you for real-world IT work. True to our mission of giving you extra, valuable knowledge in Information and Communication Technology, our events are perfect opportunities to pick up high-demand tech skills, check out the latest digital trends, and build your leadership and ethical teamwork skills. You can look forward to an exciting lineup of interactive seminars, coding or design workshops, and skill-driven challenges designed to boost your digital literacy and tech-savviness.
